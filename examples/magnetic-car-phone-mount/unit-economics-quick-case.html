How $6.68 is calculated

$28.1906 realized revenue after returns - $4.22859 referral fee - $4.25 fulfillment - $7.80 landed cost - $0.48 expected return loss - $4.1986 ads - $0.55 other = $6.68341 contribution

The 22.3% contribution margin divides $6.68341 by the $29.99 list price. This is a contribution model, not a net-profit or Amazon settlement statement.

Each downside changes one assumption while holding the others fixed. Both remain positive in this fixture, but the lower contribution leaves less room for costs the model has not captured. The two downside cases are not a combined worst case.

Profit is not first-order cash

1,000 units x $7.80 landed cost = $7,800 purchase order
$7,800 + $1,800 tooling + $450 setup + $2,500 working-capital buffer = $12,550

The 30% deposit is $2,340 and the pre-shipment balance is $5,460. These are payment stages within the $7,800 purchase order, not extra costs to add again.
